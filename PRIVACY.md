# Privacy Policy — syepubreader

_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

**syepubreader does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.**

- **Your files stay on your device.** EPUB/HTML/ZIP files you open are read and rendered entirely inside your browser. They are never uploaded to any server.
- **Local storage only.** To reopen the book you were reading and remember your reading position and display settings, the app stores the current book file and your settings in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB / localStorage) on your own device. You can remove them at any time with **Close book** or by clearing the site data in your browser.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no accounts, no ads.**
- **Translation.** If you use your browser's built-in page translation (e.g. Chrome “Translate”), that feature is provided by the browser vendor under its own privacy policy; syepubreader does not send content anywhere itself.
- **Browser extension (if installed).** The extension only adds an “Open in syepubreader” action. It uses the minimum permissions needed for that action and collects no data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/moonhosang/syepubreader/issues

## 개인정보 처리방침 (한국어 요약)

syepubreader 는 **어떤 개인정보도 수집·전송·판매·공유하지 않습니다.** 연 파일은 브라우저 안에서만 처리되며 서버로 올라가지 않습니다. 읽던 책·읽기 위치·화면 설정은 **사용자 기기의 브라우저 저장소**에만 보관되며 「책 닫기」나 사이트 데이터 삭제로 지울 수 있습니다. 분석·추적·쿠키·계정·광고가 없습니다. 브라우저 번역 기능은 해당 브라우저 제공사의 정책을 따릅니다.
